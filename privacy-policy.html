Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

🔒
Your privacy is complete and absolute. Thovario does not collect, transmit, store on servers, track, or sell any of your personal data or written thoughts.

1. Overview

Thovario ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up as a private, calm, and distraction-free sanctuary for your personal thoughts, notes, and reflections. We believe your words belong solely to you.

2. Data Storage & On-Device Processing

All entries, prompts, thoughts, favorites, and settings you create or configure in Thovario are stored strictly locally on your device using on-device SQLite database storage and local preferences.

  • No user accounts or sign-ins are required.
  • Your entries are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service operated by us.
  • We have no access to your journal entries, thoughts, or metadata.

3. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, location data, usage statistics, diagnostic telemetry, or behavioral tracking identifiers.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Thovario contains:

  • No advertising networks or trackers
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No third-party data brokers

5. Data Deletion and Backups

Because your content is only stored on your device:

  • You have complete control over your data. You can edit or delete individual entries directly inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app from your device permanently removes the local database and stored content, unless backed up by your operating system's personal device backup (such as Apple iCloud Backup).

6. Children's Privacy

Thovario does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children or any other users, as no user information is ever collected or transmitted.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
